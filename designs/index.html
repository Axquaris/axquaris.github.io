Redesign candidates

Light-mode directions for the homepage. Each is a single self-contained HTML file with its CSS in a <style> block and the same content, so they can be compared directly and the winner folded back into index.html plus stylesheet.css.

Round three — current

  1. 11Rosette

    Ledger's two-column shell — sticky identity card, pedigree badges and interests beside white content panels — with Tabula's rosette glyph as the identity mark. Two columns above 900px, one below.

Round two — blends

Scannable panels, the chalk / forest / chartreuse palette and bibliography-style publication rows, recombined five ways. These carry the institution badges and drop the experience section.

  1. 06Folio

    White panels on chalk under a forest name banner, with an acid keyword block in the research panel. The most straightforward blend.

  2. 07Tabula

    Poster header tiles, then each section hung off an acid file tab. Numbered index entries and wide monospace micro-typography.

  3. 08Strata

    One rounded sheet of stacked colour bands: forest identity, white about, sage pedigree, white research, paper publications.

  4. 09Ledger

    A sticky forest identity card with the pedigree badges stacked underneath it, beside a column of white content panels.

  5. 10Rotunda

    An acid masthead over one card per paper, each with an arch-framed figure and a chalk strip holding the venue and links.

Round one — first directions

  1. 01Lattice

    Swiss grid. Hairline rules, a monospace metadata rail down the left, no cards and no shadows. The quietest option and the closest relative of the current site.

  2. 02Atelier

    Soft white panels on a tinted ground, headshot, pill links, publication rows as cards. The most conventional academic homepage, in the vein of the Jiahui Lei reference.

  3. 03Orbit

    Graphic poster. Arch, colour blocking in chartreuse on forest, wide monospace micro-typography and a numbered index of work. The boldest of the five.

  4. 04Preprint

    The page set as a typeset paper. Monospace throughout, numbered sections, running head, figure captions and a bibliography-style list with Garamond titles.

  5. 05Manifold

    Editorial. A didone display face, violet accents, a two-column grid of work and a canvas-generated rosette with six-fold symmetry in the hero.
